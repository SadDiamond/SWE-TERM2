FOCUS AREA: The Object-Oriented Paradigm

DUE DATE:

Term 2, Week 9 (submission of code and documentation)

Term 2, Week 10 (individual presentations)

TASK DESCRIPTION:

Students use Python (or another suitable class oriented programming language), and an object-oriented programming approach, to create a computer game based inspired by a text-based role-playing adventure game originally written in an imperative or procedural language. They do this by modifying, improving, and making a unique game from a guided solution that is based loosely on the famous vintage classic Hunt the Wumpus. Student are required to modify the code so that they make an original scenario, characters, and engaging gameplay. 

Students may seek to implement a GUI and discuss other suitable modifications with their teacher. 

Alternatively, students may choose an individual project, in collaboration with their teacher, that explores OOP concepts through design of a desktop application or similar system (library management system, finance management system/tracker, hotel booking system etc.)

To accomplish this goal of creating an original game, they must

•	modify/create the classes, objects, attributes, and methods of the guided solution

•	provide documentation including a journal and modelling diagrams

•	present your solution to the class including a question and answer (Q&A) segment.
